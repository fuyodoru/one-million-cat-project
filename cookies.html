ABOUT THIS PAGE

The current application does not provide a separate optional advertising or analytics cookie-consent system. This page explains the types of technical storage that may be used while the application operates.

TECHNICAL STORAGE

The application and its authentication system may use browser or WebView storage, cached data, or similar technical mechanisms to keep the application functioning and maintain an anonymous session.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Services used by the application, such as Supabase and OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, may use their own technical mechanisms. Their own policies and settings apply to those services.

OPTIONAL TRACKING

If the application later introduces optional analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies that require user choice, this page should be updated together with an appropriate preference control.

YOUR CHOICES

Because the current application does not include a separate optional cookie preference manager, this page is informational rather than an interactive consent panel.

CHANGES

This page may be updated if the application's storage or tracking practices change.
